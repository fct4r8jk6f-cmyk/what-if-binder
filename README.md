# The What-If Binder / Le Classeur Au Cas Où

If something happened to you tonight, could your family find what they'd need?

A guided family emergency binder in 15 sections — key people, medical, money,
insurance, home, digital life, legal documents, final wishes — that prints to a
clean PDF with a one-page **Heir Access Sheet** to keep with your will.

- **English:** https://fct4r8jk6f-cmyk.github.io/what-if-binder/
- **Français (Québec):** https://fct4r8jk6f-cmyk.github.io/what-if-binder/le-classeur-au-cas-ou.html
  — liquidateur, mandat de protection, notaire, RRQ/RAMQ: written for Québec, not translated.

## Private by design
Your answers never leave your device. Each edition is one self-contained HTML
file: no account, no server, no analytics, no network requests. Answers are
saved in your browser, with **Save a backup** / **Restore** and **Print** built in.

> **iPhone / iPad:** Safari erases what a website has saved if you don't open
> it for about a week. Tap **Save a backup** each time you finish, and print
> the binder once it's complete.

## Record where secrets live — never the secrets themselves
The binder asks *where* things are ("the master password is in the envelope in
the fire safe"), not the passwords, PINs or codes.

---

An organizational tool, not legal, financial, or medical advice. For wills,
powers of attorney / mandats de protection, and estate questions, talk to a
lawyer or notary where you live.
